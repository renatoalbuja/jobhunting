ATS & Resume Tailoring Handbook

Why ATS-Proofing Matters

Over 98% of Fortune 500 companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter resumes before a human recruiter ever sees them. Resumes that are poorly formatted, use complex designs, or miss key keywords are rejected automatically.

Font & Styling Rules
  • Use Standard Fonts: Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman, Garamond, and Georgia are widely supported. Avoid custom downloaded web fonts.
  • No Text Inside Boxes: Don't place text in graphical shapes or custom borders; ATS cannot read them.
  • Keep Font Sizes Readable: 10-12pt for body text, 14-16pt for section headers, 20-24pt for your name.
Format Restrictions
  • Single Column: ATS parses left-to-right. Multi-column templates often result in merged text (e.g. reading experience side-by-side with education).
  • No Tables or Charts: ATS frequently strips tables or reads content out of order. Avoid grids or visual skill bars.
  • Standard Headers: Use "Professional Experience", "Education", "Skills", "Summary". Don't use creative headers like "My Professional Path" or "Things I'm Good At".
The STAR Methodology

For each bullet point in your Experience, use the STAR format to prove your capabilities:

  • S/T (Situation/Task): What challenge or project did you tackle?
  • A (Action): What did YOU do specifically? (Start with a strong verb).
  • R (Result): What was the outcome, backed by tangible numbers?

Example: "Boosted team productivity by 25% (Result) by implementing an automated linting and CI/CD workflow (Action) that reduced PR review times (Situation/Task)."

Keeping a Human Tone
  • Avoid AI Clichés: Overused LLM words like spearheaded, revolutionized, synergy, pioneered trigger recruiter skepticism.
  • Be Direct: Write active sentences. Say "Reduced database query time..." instead of "I was responsible for optimizing database queries..."
  • Customize Metrics: AI will generate placeholders (e.g., `[X]%`). Replace these with your actual metrics before saving!
